Lin Mu穆林

Associate Professor
School of Computer Science and Technology, Anhui University

I received my Ph.D. degree from the University of Science and Technology of China in April 2021. My research interests include LLM reasoning, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and natural language processing. I have published over 30 papers in top venues including ACL, WWW, EMNLP, IEEE TAI, IEEE TCSS, IEEE TCE, and IEEE TBD. I serve on the Program Committee of NeurIPS, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, AAAI, and SIGIR.

Research Interests

🧠

LLM Reasoning

Enhancing reasoning capabilities of large language models, including multi-hop reasoning, event causality identification, and robust prompting strategies against adversarial attacks.

⚡

Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning

Developing efficient LLM adaptation methods such as DenseLoRA, GraphLoRA, TalkLoRA, and PolyLoRA that reduce training costs while achieving competitive or superior performance.

✏️

Model / Knowledge Editing

Investigating techniques to update factual knowledge in pre-trained language models without full retraining, with a focus on controlling ripple effects and preserving model integrity.
